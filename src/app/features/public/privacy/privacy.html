Beehivemind is a beekeeping records system. Almost everything we hold is data you deliberately entered about your own hives, and it stays yours. This page explains the rest.

What we collect

Your account

Your name, email address and country. The country is optional and we ask for it only to set sensible defaults. If you sign in with Google we receive your name, email and profile picture from Google and nothing else.

Your beekeeping records

Apiaries, their coordinates, beehives, groups, inspections, feedings, harvests, treatments, costs and to-dos. Apiary coordinates are a location you place on a map yourself — the app does not track your device.

Voice recordings

Speech recognition during an inspection runs on your phone. Audio is processed on the device and is not uploaded; what reaches our servers is the recognised inspection data, not the recording. Recordings are held on the device only until the inspection is uploaded.

Technical data

When you use the website or the app we log the request, the time and an approximate location derived from your IP address, so we can keep the service running and investigate faults.

Why we hold it

  • To provide the service. Your records exist so the app can show them back to you, chart them and sync them between your phone and the website.
  • To keep your account secure. Sign-in history and two-factor settings.
  • To bill you, if you are on a paid plan. Payment details are handled by our payment provider; we never see or store your card number.

The specific lawful basis for each of these under the GDPR needs to be stated here, and confirmed by a specialist, before publication.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your hive records with other beekeepers, associations or authorities.
  • We do not use your inspection data to advertise to you or to anyone else.

Cookies and analytics

Two kinds of storage exist on this site. The first keeps you signed in and remembers the choice you made below: a cookie for the session, and an entry in your browser's own storage for the choice. The site cannot work without them and neither is ever used to follow you anywhere else. The second is Google Analytics, which tells us which pages beekeepers read and where they give up. It sets cookies named _ga and _ga_<id> that last up to two years, records the pages you visit, roughly where you are from your IP address and which site sent you, and, if you are signed in, the account number so we can tell one person's week from a thousand separate visits. It never receives your name, your email or anything from your hive records, and we run no advertising and no advertising cookies.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland nothing analytical is set until you accept it. Everywhere else it is on unless you turn it off. Either way the choice is yours to change whenever you like, and refusing changes nothing about how the site behaves.

Who else touches it

A small number of providers process data on our behalf: hosting, email delivery, error reporting, website analytics (Google Analytics), and the map provider that renders apiary locations. Each is bound by a processing agreement and may use the data only to provide their service to us.

The named list of processors and their locations belongs here. It has to be accurate, so it needs filling in from the actual infrastructure rather than assumed.

How long we keep it

Your records are kept for as long as your account is open, because beekeeping data is only useful across seasons. If you delete your account we remove your personal data and your records; backups age out afterwards on a fixed schedule.

The retention and backup periods need real numbers, taken from the infrastructure, not placeholders.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. You can export your records at any time from your account, and delete your account yourself from the profile page.

To exercise any of these, write to us at info@beehivemind.tech or through the contact page. You also have the right to complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.

Children

Beehivemind is a tool for a working business and is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell you by email when the change is significant.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your data, go to our support portal and we will answer them.
