1. The service

Beehivemind is a record-keeping system for beekeepers: apiaries, hives, inspections, feedings, harvests, treatments, costs and reminders, recorded by voice or by hand and synchronised between your phone and the website.

It is a tool for keeping records. It does not give veterinary, agricultural or financial advice, and a reading it shows you is whatever was recorded, not a diagnosis.

2. Your account

Eligibility

You need to be 16 or over and able to enter a contract. One account belongs to one person; a cooperative with several beekeepers uses several accounts under one operation rather than sharing one.

Keeping it secure

You are responsible for your password and for anything done through your account. Two-factor authentication is available and we recommend it, particularly on an account that other people can see. Tell us promptly if you think someone else has access.

Accuracy

The email address on the account has to be one you can read, because it is how we reach you about billing and about changes to these terms.

3. Plans and payment

The free plan

One apiary and up to ten hives, free, with no time limit and no card required. It is not a trial that expires.

Paid plans

Paid plans are billed monthly in advance. Prices are shown on the plans page and include or exclude VAT as stated there.

Needs real values: the currency, whether prices are VAT-inclusive, the payment provider by name, and what happens to a payment that fails — how many retries, and how long before the account changes state.

Changing plan

You can move up or down at any time from your profile. Moving up takes effect immediately and is charged pro rata; moving down takes effect at the end of the month you have paid for.

Coupons

A coupon from a cooperative or an event applies at checkout, for the period stated on it, and cannot be combined with another.

Refunds

The refund position needs a decision before it can be written, and EU consumer law sets a floor on it — a customer in the EU has a fourteen-day right of withdrawal on a digital service unless they have waived it explicitly.

4. Your records are yours

Everything you enter about your own beekeeping stays yours. We hold it so the service can show it back to you. We do not sell it, we do not share your hive records with other beekeepers or with authorities, and we do not use them to advertise.

You can export every inspection, harvest, feeding and cost you have entered, on every plan including the free one, at any time.

Going down a plan never deletes a record. If you move to a plan whose limits your data exceeds, the apiaries over the limit become read-only until you remove one or move back up. They are not erased.

One number needed: how long a closed account's data is retained before deletion, and how long backups hold it after that. The privacy page needs the same figure and it has to be the same figure.

5. What you give us permission to do

To run the service we need a limited licence to store, copy and display your records — that is what showing you your own data on two devices requires. It goes no further than operating Beehivemind for you, and it ends when you delete the data or the account.

Speech recognition during an inspection runs on your phone. Audio is not uploaded, so we take no licence over recordings.

If aggregated, fully anonymised data is ever to be used for research, benchmarking or product improvement, that has to be stated here explicitly and separately — and a beekeeper needs to be able to say no to it. Silence is not consent.

  • Resell access to Beehivemind, or rebuild it from an automated extraction of it.

Reading your own data out through the export or the API is not extraction. That is the point of it.

7. Availability and support

We keep the service running and fix faults, but we do not promise it will never be down. Maintenance happens, and the mobile app is built to keep recording without a connection precisely because signal in an apiary is not something either of us controls.

Whether a paid plan carries an uptime commitment or a support response time is a commercial decision. If it does, the figure goes here; if it does not, say so plainly rather than leaving it open.
