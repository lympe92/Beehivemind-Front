1. The service

BeehiveMind is a record-keeping system for beekeepers: apiaries, hives, inspections, feedings, harvests, treatments, costs and reminders, recorded by voice or by hand and synchronised between your phone and the website.

It is a tool for keeping records. It does not give veterinary, agricultural or financial advice, and a reading it shows you is whatever was recorded, not a diagnosis.

2. Your account

Eligibility

You need to be 16 or over and able to enter a contract. One account belongs to one person. Several beekeepers who work the same apiaries use a team, with an account each, rather than sharing one.

Teams

An account can invite other people to work on its apiaries. The account that invites is the team's owner; everyone who accepts an invitation is a team member, with their own account and their own acceptance of these terms. Only the owner invites and removes members.

  • The owner's plan covers the whole team. Members do not pay separately and have no plan of their own.
  • The owner decides who is in the team and is responsible for having invited them. Every member can see and change all of the team's records.
  • Removing a member deletes that member's account. The records they added stay with the team.
  • If the owner deletes their account, the team goes with it: its records and every member's account.

Keeping it secure

You are responsible for your password and for anything done through your account. Two-factor authentication is available and we recommend it, particularly on an account that other people can see. Tell us promptly if you think someone else has access.

Accuracy

The email address on the account has to be one you can read, because it is how we reach you about billing and about changes to these terms.

4. Your records are yours

Everything you enter about your own beekeeping stays yours. We hold it so the service can show it back to you. We do not sell it, we do not share your hive records with other beekeepers or with authorities, and we do not use them to advertise.

You can export every inspection, harvest, feeding and cost you have entered, on every plan including the free one, at any time.

In a team, the records are the team's. What a member adds belongs to the team and its owner, and stays with the team when the member leaves. Every member can export the team's records while they are in it.

Going down a plan never deletes a record. If you move to a plan whose limits your data exceeds, the apiaries over the limit become read-only until you remove one or move back up. They are not erased.

Deleting is immediate. When an account is deleted its data is removed from the live service at once. Copies can remain in our backups for up to 4 weeks, after which they are deleted automatically. The privacy policy gives the same figures.

5. What you give us permission to do

To run the service we need a limited licence to store, copy and display your records — that is what showing you your own data on two devices requires. It goes no further than operating BeehiveMind for you, and it ends when you delete the data or the account.

Speech recognition during an inspection runs on your phone. Audio is not uploaded, so we take no licence over recordings.

We do not use your records for research or benchmarking, and we do not use them to train AI models. If we ever want to use aggregated, anonymised data for any of that, we will ask first, and it will be something you choose to join — never something you have to opt out of.

One thing we do: to check that the AI assistant answers well, we review some of its answers. A reviewed answer, with the question it answered, can be sent to an AI provider for automated scoring. The privacy policy names the provider.
